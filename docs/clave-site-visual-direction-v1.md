# Clave — visual direction v0.2

Revised after the latest direction. This is a working design brief, not final copy.

**Historical draft.** The owner selected the first Black Gold Gallery assembly as the active direction. Use `clave-site-visual-direction-v3.md`; v2's cardboard archive was explicitly rejected.

## Business and audience

Clave is a full-cycle AI film production studio, ready to create any video format. The site speaks equally to direct brand clients and advertising agencies. Project budget range for planning: 100,000–1,500,000 ₽.

The studio's identity is broad neuroproduction. Do not give corporate learning or any other single secondary format its own visual emphasis on the main page.

## Concept pages

1. **Home:** hero showreel, selected films, director-led process, studio contact.
2. **Work:** curated film gallery and project pages.
3. **Services:** commercials, brand films, campaigns and series, custom video formats. Keep formats flexible; do not imply that Clave only handles a narrow list.
4. **Studio:** creative direction, production approach, team and delivery standards.
5. **Project page:** film, brief, idea, visual language, production approach, deliverables and verified outcome.

## Case-study format

- Project title and client, only when approved for public use
- Business or communication task
- Idea and visual direction
- Production approach: generative, live-action, or hybrid
- Deliverables and formats
- Outcome, only where it is documented and approved for publication

## Visual system: Archive of Stories

- Near-black base, warm paper and restrained antique-gold accents.
- An archive case becomes a miniature cinema and reusable 3D hero object.
- A small key or keyhole is the brand motif; avoid repetitive decorative keys.
- Case films appear as different worlds inside a consistent gallery frame.
- The 1880s oilfield is a single film world from the supplied reference, not the studio's overall identity.
- Use gold for labels, fine edges and focal points; keep text high-contrast and readable.
- Editorial display type for headlines, clear sans-serif for body copy, compact archive labels for metadata.

## Motion and 3D direction

- Opening sequence: the archive lid or aperture reveals a film still or muted showreel.
- On scroll: case frames or drawers move forward briefly, then settle before their text appears.
- Case transitions use a controlled camera move or light change through the archive object.
- Keep messages and actions available without waiting for animation; support reduced motion.
- Reuse one 3D kit across the hero, project gallery and case pages.

## Bilingual behavior

- Persistent RU / EN switch.
- Translate for meaning and tone, not word-for-word.
- Keep navigation, service names, CTA labels and case metadata consistent between languages.
